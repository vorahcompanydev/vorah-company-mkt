One-sentence: Brand building blocks — the recolorable Logo lockup and the eyebrow+serif SectionHeading that opens every section.

```jsx
<Logo size={28} tone="ink" />
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Nosso diferencial"
  title="Marketing com estratégia, leveza e parceria"
  intro="Unimos estratégia + execução, com foco em resultados."
/>
```

Logo tones: `ink | cream | amber`; set `showCompany={false}` for the bare wordmark. SectionHeading: `align`, `tone` (`ink|cream`), `maxWidth`. For pixel-exact marketing use the PNGs in `assets/`.
